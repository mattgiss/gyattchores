Why it exists

A bowling alley doesn't ask who you are before it lets you play. It puts your name on a screen and keeps score. That's all a house full of chores needs too.

DOYOURWORK started as one family's scoreboard, and that's still what it is: names, chores, points and a weekly winner, on a phone or tablet the household shares. There's no account to make, no email to hand over, and no database on our side holding your family's names. Meet the family behind it.

How a day works

Small enough to actually happen.

  1. Pick your 25 minutes

    Choose the chores you'd rather do, or let the app suggest a card for you.

  2. Check them off

    Tap each chore as you finish it, then send the card.

  3. An admin says yes or no

    Usually a parent, behind their own code, so nobody approves their own work.

  4. Points land

    Approved chores earn points, your streak grows, and the week's leaderboard moves.

Dares

Dare them to do it.

Every house has a regular. One person always takes the bins out, another always does the dishes. In DOYOURWORK, everyone else can dare them to, and when they show up, both of them score.

Make a dare

Pick someone and a chore you think they'll do today. You get three dares a day, and they're secret: nobody sees them until midnight.

They show up

The regular does their chore like always. Now it's a chore people were counting on, and every dare on it pays them 5 points.

You both win

If the chore got done and approved, the dare lands and you earn 10. The next morning everyone sees who dared whom, and who came through.

Being counted on is a reason to keep going. Dares turn "it's always me" into points, and doing what's expected into something the whole house notices.

Better with everyone on their own phone. Start on one phone or a shared tablet. When everyone in the family or group has DOYOURWORK on their own phone, dares unlock. iPhones and Android phones can share one family.

The reward

Do your work. Then play.

There's a game hidden inside the app, and the only way in is to send today's card. Finish your chores and Clear the Grid opens: a puzzle of pink and black blocks that gets harder as you level up.

  • Up to an hour of play a day, counted only while the game is on screen. It starts over at midnight.
  • Chores stop being the thing standing between you and the fun part, and become the way to it.
  • Over time, the app means points, being counted on, and a game you earned, for kids and grown-ups alike.
Private by design

We can't read your data. Nobody can but you.

Most apps promise to look after what they collect. This one can't see what's in it, so there's nothing to look after.

No accounts

Open it and start. No email, no password, no sign-in with anything.

Stays on your phones

On one phone or tablet, the app never goes online. Add a second phone and everything is encrypted on your phone before it's sent, with a key only your family's phones have.

Yours to keep, or erase

Save a backup to a file you control and restore it on a new phone. Erase a phone, or delete your family from the server with one tap. The server only ever held scrambled copies.

No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. The full detail is in the privacy policy, which is short.

Your chores, in your words

Just say what needs doing.

Every home is different, so the list is yours. Start from 27 everyday chores, or describe your own: type it, or tap the microphone on the keyboard and say it out loud.

"Wash the car, walk the dog for 15 minutes, feed the fish and tidy the playroom" becomes a list. Untick what you don't want, fix a name, change the minutes, and add it.

The sorting happens on your device. What you write or say isn't sent anywhere by the app.

What keeps it going

Fair, competitive, hard to argue with.

Skipped chores come back bigger

A chore nobody has done for a few days takes longer next time and moves up the list, so the unpopular ones stop being ignored.

Streaks multiply

Hit your day, keep your streak. A streak multiplies the points you earn, up to double at ten days.

Levels read like versions

You're not level 14, you're v2.3. Every approved chore ships a slightly better version of you.

One chore, one person, per day

Once someone has a chore in, it's theirs until tomorrow. No arguments over who really did the dishes.

A week and a month at a glance

A weekly leaderboard names a winner. A 30-day log shows, honestly, who has been showing up.

Dares pay both ways

The person who dared earns 10 when it happens; the person who did it earns 5 for every dare on their chore. Dare points count toward the weekly winner.

Questions

Fair questions.

When is it out?

Now, on iPhone and iPad: get it on the App Store. Android is in testing and coming to Google Play soon. Join the beta to try it first.

How do dares work?

On your own phone, tap Make a dare, pick a person and a chore you think they'll do today. Up to three a day, and you can't dare someone once they've sent their card. Dares stay secret until midnight. If they did the chore and an admin approved it, you get 10 points and they get 5 for each dare on it.

Won't the game just be more screen time?

It's capped at an hour a day, and it only opens after today's chores are sent. The game is the reward for doing your part, not a way around it.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

What do you collect about my family?

Nothing we can read. On one phone, nothing leaves it. With more than one phone, the app sends encrypted copies through our server, which sees scrambled data and an anonymous ID for each phone, never names or chores. An admin can delete it all from the server at any time. The privacy policy says the same thing at slightly greater length.

Can we each use our own phone?

Yes, and it's the best way to play: dares unlock once everyone has their own phone. In Admin, tap Add a phone, and scan the code it shows with the other phone, inside DOYOURWORK. A new phone can join right from its first screen. iPhones and Android phones can share one family.

iPhone or Android?

Both. iPhone and iPad are on the App Store now; Android is in beta and coming to Google Play. iPhones and Android phones can share one family.

What if we get a new phone?

Save a backup from the admin area. It's a file you keep wherever you like, and it restores the whole scoreboard on the new phone, even if you've switched between iPhone and Android.

What if I forget the admin code?

The code is kept only on your device, so nobody, including us, can look it up or reset it. A second admin can give you a new one, and you can let your device's face or fingerprint unlock open the admin area. Failing both, the way back in is to start a new scoreboard. Making a second person an admin on day one is the best protection.

Is it only for families with children?

No. It's for any household that shares chores: families, couples, housemates. Anyone who can read a list and tap a checkbox can play.

Does this website track me?

No. This page loads nothing from anyone else: no analytics, no cookies, no fonts or scripts from other companies. If you email us about the beta, we use your address to reply and to send the invite.
