why it exists

It started as one family's scoreboard. A bowling alley doesn't ask who you are before it lets you play. It puts your name on a screen and keeps score. That's all a house full of chores needs too.

So that's what this is. Names, chores, points and a weekly winner, on a phone or tablet the household shares. There is no account to make, no email to hand over and no database on our side holding your family's names.

how a day works

Small enough to actually happen.

  1. Pick your 25 minutes

    Choose the chores you'd rather do, or tap suggest and the app fills your card for you.

  2. Check them off

    Tap each chore as you finish it, then submit the card.

  3. An admin approves

    Usually a parent, behind their own code, so nobody approves their own work.

  4. Points land

    Approved chores earn XP, your streak grows, and the week's leaderboard moves.

private by design

We can't lose your data. We never have it.

Most apps promise to look after what they collect. This one doesn't collect anything, so there's nothing to look after.

01No accounts

Open it and start. No email, no password, no sign-in with anything.

02Stays on your device

The app has no servers and never goes online. Names, chores and points live on your iPhone or iPad and nowhere else.

03Gone when you say so

Tap erase everything, or delete the app, and it's all gone. There's no copy for us to delete, because there never was one.

No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. The full detail is in the privacy policy, which is short.

your chores, in your words

Just say what needs doing.

Every home is different, so the list is yours. Start from 27 everyday chores, or describe your own: type it, or tap the microphone on the keyboard and say it out loud.

The app sorts what you said into a list. Untick what you don't want, fix a name, change the minutes, and add it. You can add, rename, re-time and remove chores whenever you like.

The sorting happens on your device. What you write or say isn't sent anywhere by the app.

what keeps it going

Fair, a little competitive, hard to argue with.

Skipped chores come back bigger

A chore nobody has done for a few days takes longer next time and moves up the list, so the unpopular ones stop being ignored.

Streaks multiply

Hit your day, keep your streak. A streak multiplies the XP you earn, up to double at ten days.

Levels read like versions

You're not level 14, you're v2.3. Every approved chore ships a slightly better version of you.

One chore, one person, per day

Once someone has a chore in, it's theirs until tomorrow. No arguments over who really did the dishes.

A week and a month at a glance

A weekly leaderboard names a winner. A 30-day log shows, honestly, who has been showing up.

A game for finishing

Submit your card and a puzzle unlocks: CLEAR THE GRID, 200 levels. Done early? A bonus round takes extra chores too.

one device, one scoreboard

Put it where the household can see it.

GyattChores runs on a single iPhone or iPad that everyone shares: the tablet in the kitchen, or a parent's phone. On iPad the scoreboard and the day's card sit side by side.

  • Admins run it. Make anyone an admin, each with their own code. Admins approve chores and manage players and the chore list.
  • Players keep it fun. Everyone can give themselves a display name. The sillier the better.
  • Not yet: a scoreboard shared between several phones. That's planned, using your family's own iCloud, so it still won't pass through us.

questions

Fair questions.

When is it out?

Soon. It's in testing now and headed to the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Join the beta and you'll be among the first to know.

What does it cost?

The beta is free. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

What do you collect about my family?

Nothing. The app has no accounts and no code that connects to the internet. Everything you put into it stays on your device. The privacy policy says the same thing at slightly greater length.

Can we each use our own phone?

Not yet. Today it's one shared scoreboard on one iPhone or iPad. Sharing a scoreboard between a household's devices is planned, through your own iCloud.

Is there an Android version?

It's in the works. iPhone and iPad come first.

What if I forget the admin code?

The code is kept only on your device, so nobody, including us, can look it up or reset it. A second admin can give you a new one, and you can let Face ID or Touch ID open the admin area. Failing both, deleting the app starts a new scoreboard. Making a second person an admin on day one is the best protection.

Is it only for families with children?

No. It's for any household that shares chores: families, couples, housemates. Anyone who can read a list and tap a checkbox can play.

Does this website track me?

No. This page loads nothing from anyone else: no analytics, no cookies, no fonts or scripts from other companies. If you email us about the beta, we use your address to reply and to send the invite.
