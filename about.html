About

Made by a family, for families.

Four of us build DOYOURWORK, and we use it every day.

Who we are

I'm Matthew. I work in IT (my portfolio is at gissentanna.com), and I build things for my family on the side. Andrea, our 14-year-old daughter and our 12-year-old son are the rest of the team: they test every version, argue about the rules, and tell me when something is boring.

DOYOURWORK is how our house keeps track of what needs doing. It's private, because a list of who did the dishes is nobody's business but ours. And when something comes up, like the garage needing a clean-out before the weekend, we say it out loud and it's a list in seconds. The kids can see their help add up instead of disappearing into "thanks."

How it started

It began in December 2025 as GyattChores, a weekend project with my kids. The first version was a single web page on a tablet on the wall: two players, a list of chores and a weekly total. It worked, and then it got interesting.

We built it the way I build things at work, in short sprints, each one fixing what the last one taught us:

  • The game. After the first week the novelty wore off, so we added a weekly winner, streaks and levels.
  • The wall tablet. Real use showed what a kitchen screen needs: big tap targets, and a board you can read from across the room.
  • Fairness. The same easy chores kept getting picked, so skipped chores started coming back bigger.
  • Security. Even a family tool deserves to be locked down properly. That sprint changed how we thought about all of it.

What it became

In 2026 we rebuilt it from scratch as an app for iPhone, iPad and Android, around one rule: we should never be able to see your family's data. No accounts, nothing stored on our side that we can read, and encrypted sharing between your own phones. The name changed to DOYOURWORK, and on 19 November 2026 it went live on the App Store.

It's still the app we use at home. If something in it bugs your family, it has probably bugged ours too, and we'd like to hear about it.
