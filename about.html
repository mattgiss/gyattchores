GYATTCHORES  ·  A FAMILY PROJECT

What this is

GyattChores is a chore tracking app built for one family. Each day a kid builds a card of about 25 minutes of work — they pick the chores they want, and the app suggests more to fill the time. When they're done, they submit the card and dad approves it. XP accumulates and your level climbs. Neglected chores escalate and get suggested first.

The idea is simple: do a little every day so nothing piles up into a big chore. Kids choose what to tackle, the app keeps the day at ~25 minutes, and keeping a daily streak builds momentum that multiplies the XP you earn. Skipping makes tomorrow heavier.

Pomodoro for kids. 25 minutes of focused work, every day.

How we built it

Built over weekends by Matthew Gissentanna with feedback from the kids. The feature set came from actual use — not a roadmap. The one-card-per-day rule was invented after someone claimed the same chore three times in one day. The daily card came from asking why they weren't using the app unprompted — and letting kids pick their own chores came from one who didn't want them chosen for her.

The app is a single HTML file with no build step. React and Supabase are loaded from CDN. Edit the file, push to GitHub, done.

Design principles

  • Let kids choose, then help — they pick their chores and the app suggests more to reach 25 minutes
  • Momentum is the reward — keep a daily streak and your XP multiplies while chores stay light
  • No accounts, no personal data, no third-party tracking
  • Single file, no build tools — change it in 60 seconds
  • Parent approval is non-negotiable — XP doesn't count until checked
