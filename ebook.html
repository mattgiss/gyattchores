How it works

GyattChores is a daily chore card system. Every day, each player gets a card of 3 chores targeting 25 minutes of focused work. Check them all off, submit the card, wait for approval. Points accumulate. Neglected chores escalate and surface first on the next card.

The goal isn't to pick chores — it's to do what's on your card. The app tells you what matters right now.

For kids — using the daily card

  1. Open the app — tap your name on the player select screen
  2. See your card — 3 chores are waiting, estimated 25 minutes total
  3. Check them off — tap a chore row to mark it done
  4. Submit when complete — the submit button activates once all 3 are checked
  5. Wait for approval — a parent reviews and approves or rejects each chore
  6. Points land — approved chores add points to your total

Your card is set for the day

The same card shows all day. It's generated in the morning based on which chores have been neglected the longest. Finish it and you're done until tomorrow.

For parents — approving chores

  1. Tap GYATTCHORES 5 times quickly on the player select screen to open admin
  2. Enter code — 7874
  3. Review pending chores — each submitted chore appears with player name and timestamp
  4. Approve or reject — APPROVE gives the points, REJECT sends it back without triggering escalation

Approve promptly

Quick feedback keeps kids motivated. A rejected chore means they can try again — explain why it didn't pass so they know what to fix.

Escalation system

Every chore tracks the last time it was approved. The longer a chore goes without being done, the more time it costs on the card — and the higher it surfaces in the next day's assignment.

Consistency is the reward

Keep up with your card every day and chores stay at their base time. Fall behind and they get heavier. The incentive is a lighter card tomorrow, not more points.

Admin access

  • How to open: Tap "GYATTCHORES" title 5 times within 2 seconds on the player select screen
  • Code: 7874
  • Filter views: pending / approved / rejected / all
  • Approve: confirms the chore was done, awards points
  • Reject: marks it rejected, no points, no escalation impact

Troubleshooting

Card not updating

The card is cached for the day. If something looks wrong, clear the browser's local storage and reload. On iOS Safari: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data → gyattchores.com → Delete.

Points not appearing after approval

The app syncs every 5 seconds. Wait a moment and the player's point total will update. If offline, it syncs when the connection returns.

Submit button not activating

All 3 chores on the card must be checked before submit activates. Check each row — a chore row turns green with a [+] when marked.

App shows "offline"

The app works without a connection — logs save locally and sync when back online. The offline indicator in the top-right corner turns green once the Supabase connection is restored.
