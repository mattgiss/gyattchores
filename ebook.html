How it works

GyattChores is a daily chore card system. Every day, each player builds a card of about 25 minutes of work — pick the chores you want, then top it up with the app's suggestions. Check them all off, submit the card, wait for approval. XP accumulates and your level climbs. Neglected chores escalate and get suggested first.

The goal is to do a little every day. You choose what to tackle; the app keeps the day at ~25 minutes so nothing piles up into a big chore.

For kids — using the daily card

  1. Open the app — tap your name on the player select screen
  2. Build your card — pick the chores you want, then tap fill to 25m to top it up with suggestions
  3. Check them off — tap a chore row to mark it done
  4. Submit when complete — the submit button activates once every chore is checked
  5. Wait for approval — a parent reviews and approves or rejects each chore
  6. XP lands — approved chores add XP, and your version number climbs (v1.0 → v1.1 → …)

Your card is set for the day

Once you lock in your card it shows all day — build it however you like, with the most-neglected chores suggested first. Finish it and you're done until tomorrow. Need to change it before submitting? Tap ↻ re-pick.

For parents — approving chores

  1. Tap GYATTCHORES 5 times quickly on the player select screen to open admin
  2. Enter code — your family's private admin code
  3. Review pending chores — each submitted chore appears with player name and timestamp
  4. Approve or reject — APPROVE awards the XP, REJECT sends it back without triggering escalation

Approve promptly

Quick feedback keeps kids motivated. A rejected chore means they can try again — explain why it didn't pass so they know what to fix.

Escalation system

Every chore tracks the last time it was approved. The longer a chore goes without being done, the more time it costs on the card — and the higher it's suggested when you build the next day's card.

Consistency is the reward

Keep up with your card every day and chores stay at their base time — and your daily streak multiplies the XP you earn. Fall behind and they get heavier. The incentive is a lighter card tomorrow and a bigger multiplier today.

Levels and momentum

Approved chores earn XP, and your XP sets your level — shown as a version number, like you're shipping a better you: v1.0 → v1.1 → … → v2.0 (a new major version every 10 levels). Early levels come fast — just a few chores each — so the bar fills quickly and the rank-ups keep coming.

Momentum

Showing up is rewarded. Every consecutive day you keep your streak alive multiplies the XP your chores are worth — up to 2× at a 10-day streak. Miss a day and the multiplier resets, but your XP never goes down.

Your streak counts consecutive days where at least one chore was approved. A streak of 0 means nothing was approved yesterday.

Admin access

  • How to open: Tap "GYATTCHORES" title 5 times within 2 seconds on the player select screen
  • Code: the family's private admin code (not published here)
  • Filter views: pending / approved / rejected / all
  • Approve: confirms the chore was done, awards XP
  • Reject: marks it rejected, no XP, no escalation impact

Troubleshooting

Card not updating

The card is cached for the day. If something looks wrong, clear the browser's local storage and reload. On iOS Safari: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data → gyattchores.com → Delete.

XP not appearing after approval

The app syncs every 5 seconds. Wait a moment and the player's XP total will update. If offline, it syncs when the connection returns.

Submit button not activating

Every chore on the card must be checked before submit activates. Check each row — a chore row turns pink with a [+] when marked.

App shows "offline"

The app works without a connection — logs save locally and sync when back online. The offline indicator in the top-right corner turns green once the Supabase connection is restored.
