Privacy Policy

GYATTCHORES FOR IPHONE AND IPAD  ·  LAST UPDATED NOVEMBER 13, 2026

The short version

GyattChores for iPhone and iPad collects nothing. Everything you put into it stays on the device you put it into. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours for it to be sent to. We can't see your family's names, chores or points, and neither can anyone else.

What the app keeps, and where

To work as a scoreboard the app stores, on your device only:

  • The names and display names you type for the people in your household
  • Your chore list
  • A log of chores done: who, which chore, its points and minutes, when, and whether it was approved
  • Which people are admins, and a scrambled form of each admin code (never the code itself)
  • Settings such as sound on or off

These are files in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. Other apps can't read them.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app contains no code that connects to the internet. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no outside services of any kind.

Two things your device itself may do, under your control and not ours:

  • Backups. If you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on, Apple includes this app's files in your backup, as it does for your other apps. We have no access to your backups.
  • Dictation. If you say your chores using the microphone key on the keyboard, that is Apple's dictation, handled by your device under Apple's privacy policy. The app itself never uses the microphone and only receives the resulting text.

Children

Households with children use GyattChores, usually on a device a parent sets up. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children. Nicknames work as well as real names, and we suggest using them.

Face ID and Touch ID

An admin can choose to open the admin area with Face ID or Touch ID. This is off unless you turn it on. Your device does the check and tells the app only yes or no; the app never sees your face or fingerprint.

Deleting your data

You hold all of it, so you can delete all of it, at any time, without asking us:

  • In the app: admin → device → erase everything
  • Or delete the app from your device
  • To remove one person and their history: admin → players → edit → remove

There is nothing for us to delete, because nothing was sent to us.

The website

This policy covers the GyattChores app for iPhone and iPad. The older web version at gyattchores.com works differently and has its own policy.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by letting a household share one scoreboard between its devices, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
