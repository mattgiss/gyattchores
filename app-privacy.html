DOYOURWORK for iPhone, iPad and Android

Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

DOYOURWORK collects nothing. Everything you put into it stays on the device you put it into. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours for it to be sent to. We can't see your family's names, chores or points, and neither can anyone else.

What the app keeps, and where

To work as a scoreboard the app stores, on your device only:

  • The names and display names you type for the people in your household
  • Your chore list
  • A log of chores done: who, which chore, its points and minutes, when, and whether it was approved
  • Which people are admins, and a scrambled form of each admin code (never the code itself)
  • Settings such as sound and colour theme, and progress in the built-in puzzle

These are files in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet. Other apps can't read them.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you send it yourself. The app contains no code that connects to the internet, and on Android it doesn't hold the permission to. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no outside services of any kind.

Three things happen only under your control, not ours:

  • Backups you save. An admin can save a backup: one file holding everything in the list above. The app hands that file to your device, and you choose where it goes. Wherever you put it, it's yours to look after. We never receive it.
  • Your device's own backups. On iPhone and iPad, if you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on, Apple includes this app's files in your backup, as it does for your other apps. On Android, the app opts out of the phone's automatic backup, so its files are not included. We have no access to either.
  • Dictation. If you say your chores using the microphone key on the keyboard, that is your keyboard's voice typing (Apple's on iPhone and iPad, usually Google's on Android), handled under that company's privacy policy. The app itself never uses the microphone and only receives the resulting text.

Children

Households with children use DOYOURWORK, usually on a device a parent sets up. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children. Nicknames work as well as real names, and we suggest using them.

Face and fingerprint unlock

An admin can choose to open the admin area with Face ID or Touch ID on iPhone and iPad, or with fingerprint or face unlock on Android. This is off unless you turn it on. Your device does the check and tells the app only yes or no; the app never sees your face or fingerprint.

Deleting your data

You hold all of it, so you can delete all of it, at any time, without asking us:

  • In the app, open Admin and tap Erase everything
  • Or delete the app from your device
  • To remove one person and their history, open Admin, tap Edit next to their name, then remove them

If you saved backup files, delete those wherever you kept them. There is nothing for us to delete, because nothing was sent to us.

This website

gyattchores.com is a set of plain pages about the app. It uses no analytics, no cookies and no trackers, and loads nothing from other companies. Like any website, the company that hosts it (GitHub) sees the ordinary technical details of a visit, such as an IP address.

If you email us, for example to join the beta, we use your address to reply and to send your invite, and for nothing else. Ask and we'll delete it.

Earlier test versions

Before this version, test builds for one family kept their chores in step between devices using an online database. Those builds have their own policy. Nothing on this page describes them.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by letting a household share one scoreboard between its devices, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
