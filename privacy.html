Privacy Policy

GyattChores · Last updated June 6, 2026 · Effective immediately

What GyattChores is

GyattChores is a private family chore tracker. Children tap to log chores they have completed, a parent approves them with a PIN, and points roll into a friendly weekly competition. It is designed to be used within a single household.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data. GyattChores does not require an account, email address, name, phone number, or any identifying information.

The only information stored by the app is a list of chore log entries, each containing:

  • A player nickname chosen by your family (e.g. "bekindhearted")
  • The name of the chore completed
  • The point value of that chore
  • The date and time it was logged
  • An approval status (pending, approved, or rejected)

This information is stored on your device in local storage (browser) or UserDefaults (iOS). It never leaves your device unless you choose to enable optional cross-device sync (see below).

Optional cloud sync

GyattChores offers optional sync via Supabase so that chore logs can be shared across family members' devices. If you enable sync:

  • The chore log entries described above are stored in a private Supabase database associated with your household.
  • Access is controlled by Supabase's row-level security. No other users can access your data.
  • Supabase's own privacy policy applies to this data: supabase.com/privacy

Sync is off by default. The app works fully without it.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No names, email addresses, or accounts
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers
  • No analytics or crash reporting sent to third parties
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No data sold or shared with any third party

Children's privacy

GyattChores is designed for use by children under parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The player nicknames used in the app are chosen by the parent and are not personal data under COPPA or similar regulations. No data is shared with third parties.

Data deletion

All local data can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's storage:

  • Web app: Open browser DevTools → Application → Local Storage → Clear all entries for gyattchores.com
  • iOS app: Delete and reinstall the app, or go to Settings → General → iPhone Storage → GyattChores → Delete App
  • Cloud sync (if enabled): Contact the sync database owner (the household that set up Supabase), or delete the Supabase project.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will be updated and a summary of changes will be posted in the app's release notes.
