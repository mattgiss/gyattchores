Privacy Policy

GYATTCHORES  ·  LAST UPDATED NOVEMBER 13, 2026

In short

GyattChores is a chore tracker built by Matthew Gissentanna for one household. It covers the web app at gyattchores.com and the iOS and Android apps.

Everyone who uses GyattChores shares one chore log. It is kept in an online database so that every phone, tablet and browser shows the same thing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored

  • Usernames. The player names a parent sets up, for example "sockninja".
  • Display names. An optional nickname a player can give themselves (iOS app).
  • Chore logs. Who did which chore, the points it was worth, when it was logged, and whether it is pending, approved or rejected.
  • The player list, including which players have been removed (iOS and Android apps).
  • Dares. A player's guesses about which chores another player will do that day (iOS and Android apps).

Usernames and display names are whatever you type. Please use nicknames, not real names.

GyattChores does not ask for an email address, phone number, real name, location, contacts or photos, and it does not read device or advertising identifiers.

Where it is stored

  • On your device, so the app works without a connection. A few things never leave it: the card you are still working on, the sound setting, and which player the device belongs to.
  • In our database. Whenever the app is open and online it sends everything listed above to a database hosted by Supabase, and downloads the latest copy. Sync is always on; there is no switch to turn it off. Supabase's privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • In our family's Discord channel. An automated job, run on GitHub, checks the database every ten minutes and posts new chore submissions, approvals and rejections (username, chores and points) to our family's Discord channel, so a parent knows there is something to approve. Discord's privacy policy: discord.com/privacy

Like any internet service, the companies that carry this data can see the internet (IP) address a request comes from: Supabase for sync, and for the web app also GitHub, which hosts this site, and the unpkg and jsDelivr networks it loads code from. GyattChores does not store or use IP addresses.

Who can see it

There are no accounts, and every copy of GyattChores connects to the same database. Everyone who has the app, or opens the web app, shares one player list and one chore log, and can see and change all of it.

The parent code stops players from approving their own chores. It does not hide anything. Dares are hidden in the app until the next day, but they are stored with everything else.

This is why GyattChores is meant for one household. Do not put anything in it that you would not want the rest of the household to see.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking code
  • No selling of data, and no sharing beyond the services named above, which store and deliver it for us
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint is used only to open the parent area. Your device does the check; the app never sees it.

Children

Children use GyattChores with a parent, and a parent sets up the players. The app does not ask a child for a real name, email address, phone number, photo or location. A parent can ask to see or delete their child's data at any time, using the address below.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays in the database until we delete it. Deleting the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes only the copy on that device. The apps cannot delete what is already in the database.

To have data deleted, email matt@gissentanna.com with the username. Within 30 days we will delete that player's chore logs, player-list entry, display name and dares from the database, delete the Discord messages that mention them, and reply to confirm.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page changes with it.
